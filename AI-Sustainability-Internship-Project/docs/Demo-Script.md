# 3-Minute Demo Script

## Opening (20 seconds)
My project is WasteSense Campus. It addresses a simple but visible sustainability problem: students often want to segregate waste correctly, but they are unsure where items like coffee cup lids, used sachets, or old chargers should go.

## Problem (30 seconds)
The primary SDG alignment is SDG 12, Responsible Consumption and Production. The secondary links are SDG 11 and SDG 13 because better campus waste practices improve community sanitation and reduce avoidable emissions.

## AI solution (60 seconds)
The prototype uses AI in three ways. First, it classifies an item from a description. Second, it retrieves policy guidance from a local waste FAQ pack, simulating a RAG workflow. Third, it estimates impact using simple factors. The important part is not complexity; it is responsible decision support with confidence and cautions.

## Live demo (40 seconds)
I will classify a coffee cup lid. The system identifies likely plastic contamination risk and suggests the safer route. Then I ask a policy question about old batteries. The assistant retrieves the closest guidance and cites the local policy pack.

## Closing (30 seconds)
WasteSense Campus shows how AI can support sustainability when it is explainable, privacy-aware, and grounded in real user behaviour. The next step would be testing with one hostel block and replacing the sample logic with approved IBM model workflows and official campus documents.
