## Problem Statement
How might we use AI to help campus community members identify common waste items and choose the correct disposal route so that hostel and classroom waste can become more sustainable?

## AI Solution Overview
WasteSense Campus is a lightweight prototype that uses AI-assisted classification and retrieval to support real-world waste decisions. The demo includes:
- item classification from text or file name
- confidence scoring and ambiguity warnings
- policy Q&A using a simulated RAG approach
- simplified impact estimation for CO2e and water proxies

The prototype is designed to be explainable and conservative. It supports users at the decision moment without claiming full automation or surveillance.

## Target Users
- Students living in hostels and using shared campus bins
- Hostel wardens and cleaning staff
- Facilities and sustainability teams
- Campus environmental clubs

## Responsible AI Considerations
The project addresses fairness, transparency, privacy, and ethics. It avoids personal identification, explains recommendations, and makes clear that final responsibility remains with campus policy and human staff.

## Expected Impact
- improved segregation confidence
- reduced contamination in recyclable streams
- better awareness of repeated confusion points
- support for campus-level sustainability reporting
