# Impact Statement

## If implemented, what changes?
WasteSense Campus changes the moment of disposal from confusion to guided action. Instead of guessing, a student can describe an item and receive a clear route, caution, and explanation.

## Who benefits and how?
### Students
- less anxiety about doing the right thing
- faster disposal decisions
- better understanding of contamination and recycling limits

### Hostel wardens and facilities staff
- fewer incorrectly mixed bins
- clearer feedback on common confusion points
- better-targeted awareness campaigns

### Campus sustainability cell
- aggregate insight into waste behaviour
- support for SDG 12 reporting
- a scalable model for responsible AI awareness projects

## Measurable outcomes to track in a pilot
- percentage of bins with low contamination
- number of repeated policy questions
- participation rate across hostel blocks
- estimated kg of recyclables diverted monthly
- student confidence score before and after use
