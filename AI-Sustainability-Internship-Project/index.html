02 // SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

A Responsible AI layer between the item and the bin.

Bridging student habits and municipal recycling through transparent decision support, powered by IBM Granite prompt pipelines and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

Multi-Signal Input Ingestion

Accepts natural-language queries (e.g. "greasy aluminum food foil with cheese") alongside multimodal packaging recognition. The engine normalizes syntax and extracts material tokens.

[RAW TEXT / IMAGE] → [TOKEN EXTRACTION] → [CONFIDENCE MATRIX] → [CONFOUND CHECK]

Transparent Output Calibration

Instead of a black-box answer, WasteSense Campus yields the recommended receptacle, cleansing actions (e.g. "rinse milk residue"), confidence percentages, and explicit contamination risk warnings.

[RECOMMENDED BIN] + [PREPARATION TIP] + [CARBON PROXY] + [AUDIT LOG]

Granite 3.0 Instruct

Lightweight, enterprise-grade reasoning engine fine-tuned to classify composite materials and identify multi-layer packaging.

Granite Assistant Workflow

Autonomous workflow orchestration for converting raw campus circulars into structured vector query answers.

Edge Inference Ready

Optimized for ultra low-latency browser and campus kiosk deployment without heavy cloud subscription overhead.

Local Campus Policy Vector Pack

Demonstrating high-precision Retrieval-Augmented Generation. Rather than hallucinating waste rules, the engine anchors every response in local institutional guidelines:

  • Hostel Wardens Circular on Room E-Waste Disposal & Battery Safety (Section 4.1)
  • Campus Canteen Food Segregation & Bio-Gas Wet Digester Protocols
  • Municipal Dry Recyclables Collection Timings & Unwashed Plastic Rejections

No Surveillance or Shaming

The system guides decisions voluntarily. It does not utilize facial recognition, student identity profiling, or punitive penalization.

Explicit Uncertainty Cautions

When items possess ambiguous material boundaries (such as wax-coated paper plates), the system highlights ambiguity rather than faking 100% confidence.

06 // GOVERNANCE & ETHICS

Engineered for human trust, not punitive surveillance.

Built strictly adhering to the 1M1B Responsible AI Framework and IBM AI Ethics Pillars.

1. Fairness & Inclusivity

The classification algorithm focuses exclusively on physical packaging characteristics, avoiding any demographic, language, or socioeconomic proxies. Accessible design supports dark/light mode and screen readers.

2. Radical Transparency & Explainability

Every classification explicitly presents confidence levels and the matched keyword signals. The user is told why an item is classified, enabling them to verify and build their own knowledge.

3. Privacy & Data Minimization

Zero persistent user tracking, cookies, or facial biometrics. In a production camera kiosk, images are processed in-memory on edge nodes and discarded immediately after classification.

4. Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards

The platform serves as a decision-support copilot. It does not automate physical door locks on bins or substitute for trained facility sanitation staff inspection.
